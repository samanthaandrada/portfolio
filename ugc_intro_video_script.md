# UGC Intro Video Script
**"Hi, I'm Samantha"**
Target length: 20-25 seconds

---

## SHOT 1 — 0:00-0:03
**Visual:** Run or walk into frame, flip the camera up toward your face, big energy. Point at the lens or give a little wave.
**You say:** "Hi, I'm Samantha."
**On-screen text starts typing:** `JOB TITLE:`

---

## SHOT 2 — 0:03-0:07
**Visual:** You with a camera in hand, golden hour, somewhere gorgeous.
**You say:** "I'm a photographer..."
**On-screen text types:** `PHOTOGRAPHER`

---

## SHOT 3 — 0:07-0:10
**Visual:** You in front of a nice backdrop — something that reads "on-camera talent."
**You say:** "...sometimes a model..."
**On-screen text types:** `MODEL`

---

## SHOT 4 — 0:10-0:14
**Visual:** You reviewing content on your phone or laptop, maybe at a café or hotel.
**You say:** "...okay, also a social media strategist and content creator."
**On-screen text types:** `CONTENT CREATOR. STRATEGIST.`

---

## SHOT 5 — 0:14-0:20
**Visual:** Mix of clips — some behind the camera directing/shooting, some where you're in the frame. The visual contrast does the talking.
**You say:** "I've shot campaigns in South Africa, the Caribbean, [add yours] — for brands, agencies, and luxury hotels."
**On-screen text:** `I TRAVEL FOR THIS.`

---

## SHOT 6 — 0:20-0:25
**Visual:** Back to you, straight to camera. Confident, a little cheeky.
**You say:** "I know what looks good from both sides of the lens."
**On-screen text fades in:** `@yourhandle | NOW BOOKING`

---

## Production Notes

- **Typing text effect:** CapCut has a built-in typewriter animation that's easy to use. Canva video works too.
- **Transitions:** Keep them quick and choppy on purpose — that's what makes it feel native UGC and not like a polished ad.
- **Shot 5:** The behind/in-front mix is key. Even 2-3 seconds of you actually holding a camera or directing a shot sells the dual-skill angle immediately.
- **The closer:** "I know what looks good from both sides of the lens" is your differentiator line. It covers you whether you're booked as photographer, creator, or talent — and signals creative intelligence to brands hiring for campaign work.
