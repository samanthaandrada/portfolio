The case for UGC

Why UGC?

Brands
92%

Customers are looking for reviews and suggestions from existing customers.

87%

Brands are using UGC to share authentic content with their customers.

72%

Brands believe UGC builds brand trust when used to create connection with their customers.

Consumers
60%

Consumers believe what existing customers say about a brand more than what brands say about themselves.

64%

Consumers seek reviews about products before making a purchase.

50%

Consumers want brands to tell them what type of content to use on social media.
